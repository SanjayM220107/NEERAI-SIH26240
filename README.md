# 💧 NEERAI

### AI-Powered Spring Revival & Recharge Planning for Tribal Areas

> **Smart India Hackathon 2026 | SIH26240 | Software**

**NEERAI** is an AI-assisted GIS decision-support platform designed to help identify, analyze, prioritize, and plan recharge interventions for springs in tribal areas.

It combines **Geospatial Intelligence, Environmental Data, AI/ML, Explainable AI, Recharge Suitability Analysis, Intervention Planning, and Field Validation** into one unified platform.

---

## 🌍 The Problem

Tribal communities in hilly and remote regions often depend on natural springs for drinking water, agriculture, livestock, and livelihoods.

However, spring systems face challenges such as:

- Seasonal reduction in spring discharge
- Degradation of recharge zones
- Changing rainfall patterns
- Deforestation and land-use changes
- Limited availability of structured spring data
- Difficulty identifying priority recharge areas
- Fragmented field and environmental information
- Time-consuming manual planning

The major challenge is not only identifying a spring, but understanding:

> **Where does the spring get its water, how suitable is the surrounding area for recharge, which springs should be prioritized, and what intervention should be considered?**

---

# 💡 Our Solution — NEERAI

NEERAI converts environmental and spring-related information into an **explainable and prioritized recharge action plan**.
